Database Logistics

Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mathchi/diabetes-data-set

Description: The data referenced was orginally from the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases where the data stored referenced diagnostic measurements that would predict whether a patient would have diabetes. The patient data numerica identifiers referenced in the dataset included:

1. Pregnancies: Number of times pregnant
2. Glucose: Plasma glucose concentration a 2 hours in an oral glucose tolerance test
3. BloodPressure: Diastolic blood pressure (mm Hg)
4. SkinThickness: Triceps skin fold thickness (mm)
5. Insulin: 2-Hour serum insulin (mu U/ml)
6. BMI: Body mass index (weight in kg/(height in m)^2)
7. DiabetesPedigreeFunction: Diabetes pedigree function
8. Age: Age (years)
9. Outcome: Class variable (0 or 1) which was the outcome of the prediction which identifies if a patient has diabetes or not. 

All the patient data utilized was from females that are at least 21 years old and they are part of the Pima Indian Heritage.
